Fraser River Tunnel Project - Preparatory Construction Works

Application for a Fisheries Act Authorization (FAA) for the Fraser River Tunel Project (FRTP) Preparatory Construction Works (PCW) as submitted by the Transportation Investment Corporation (the Proponent).

Project Description

The proposed Fraser River Tunnel Project (FRTP) is a multi-year project that includes the construction of a new eight-lane, immersed tube tunnel to replace the existing George Massey Tunnel (GMT). The FRTP also includes the replacement of the existing Deas Island Bridge, the construction of Highway 99 connections north (Richmond) and south (Delta) of the tunnel, and eventual filling of the decommissioned GMT. As part of the proposed FRTP, the proponent will be required to apply for Fisheries Act Authorizations (FAA) at various stages in the FRTP development.

Fisheries Act Authorization

Fisheries and Oceans Canada (DFO) Fish and Fish Habitat Protection Program (FFHPP) role is in reviewing works, undertakings, and activities proposed within the FAA applications it receives, including determining if the works will cause harmful alteration, disruption or destruction (HADD) of fish habitat, or death of fish, and to ensure these are addressed by appropriate avoidance, mitigation and offsetting measures. Thus, the applications under DFO's review are significantly scoped down from the larger FRTP application assessed through the British Columbia (BC) Environmental Assessment (EA) process. DFO consults with First Nations on potential adverse impacts to section 35 or treaty rights which First Nations may identify from within the FAA applications.

FAA Phases

PHASE 1 - The current Application under review is for the first phase of the FRTP, the preparatory construction works only (a second application for the main tunnel construction works will be submitted later in 2026).

This first phase involves essential preparatory required to support Project construction, including temporary structures (e.g. access trestles and jetties), ground improvement, site preparation and remediation, utility and construction support infrastructure, and transportation modifications such as highway tie-ins and rail diversions.

PHASE 2 - Main Tunnel Construction Works. DFO anticipates the proponent will submit an FAA application to DFO in October 2026. DFO will conduct consultation appropriate to that application, when received.
